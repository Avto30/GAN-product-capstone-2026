# Problem 1
**Author :** Avtandili Gogoladze
**Who I watched :** My mom who lets My little brother use Youtube, Tiktok, games and a web browser on a personal phone or tablet.  

**Struggle I saw :** My mom reularly takes the device from my brother and manually checks his search history, watched videos or installed apps because she does not know what my brother has been seeing; When she finds something she does not like she either argues with my brother, removes the app completely or takes away the device.  

**COST I can name :** repeatedly checking the device, my mom being angry at my brother, my brother being upset because his phone got taken away.

# Problem 2
**Author :** Avtandili Gogoladze
**Who I watched :** My father, who owns an elecrtic car and drives between different cities several times a month.  

**Struggle I saw :** Before longer trips, he manually checks where charging stations are and has alter and take more complex routes, he even calls and messages friends to decide where to stop, because he does not know which stations on his route actually have the best prices and quality.  

**Cost I can name :** 15–20 minutes of checking before a trip, lengthy and most of the time futile phone calls, detours, and extra fuel cost when he stops at a more expensive station simply because it is the first convenient one.

# Problem 3
**Author** : Avtandili Gogoladze
**Who I watched :** My friend Gigi, who goes to the gym after university during the busiest evening hours.  

**Struggle I saw :** He changed the order of his entire workout because the machine he needed was occupied, walked around repeatedly checking whether it had become free, then eventually skipped that exercise because somebody else took it immediately after the first person finished.  

**Cost I can name :** 15–20 minutes of waiting and wandering, incomplete workouts, longer gym sessions, and frustration during almost every busy evening visit.

# Problem 4
**Author** : Nita Javelidze

**Who I watched :** My friend Mariam, a KIU student who lives far from campus.

**Struggle I saw :** She arranged most of her in-person classes into two long days each week to avoid commuting to campus for only one short lecture. On those days, she has classes from around 10:00 to 17:00 and on Capella days, stays for rehearsal at 19:00. When she has two or three hour gaps between classes, going home and returning is not practical because she lives far from campus.

**Cost I can name :** She recently had to turn down a well-paid job opportunity because the schedule conflicted with her university timetable. 

# Problem 5
**Author** : Nita Javelidze

**Who I watched :** My sister, a medical professional who is currently completing her residency and PhD while also working at a university. 

**Struggle I saw :** She received a grant to attend professional training abroad that would help her develop in her medical field, but her workplace did not approve her leave because they needed her at work during the same period. 

**Cost I can name :** She had to miss a funded professional-development opportunity that could have contributed to her career and specialization.

# Problem 6
**Author** : Nita Javelidze

**Who I watched :** My mother, a professional musician who teaches at music schools and a conservatory, while also teaching several private students.

**Struggle I saw :** Her teaching schedule takes up a large part of her week across several workplaces, while she is also trying to maintain her own career as a performing musician and prepare for concerts. I have seen her arrange her concert travel around her students' lessons because she cannot easily cancel or postpone them.

**Cost I can name :** Her teaching responsibilities leave her with limited time for her own practice and concert preparation and restrict how much time she can dedicate to performing and developing her own career.
